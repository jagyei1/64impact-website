The 64impact Approach

Then. Now. How. Action.

Every engagement follows the same framework: where a business started, where it stands today, how the change happened, and the specific actions behind it.

Then

A business relying on referrals and inconsistent enquiries, with no predictable paid acquisition channel in place.

Now
