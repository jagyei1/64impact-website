Why It Matters

Most accounts we review have a tracking problem.

Before any campaign can be optimised properly, the tracking behind it needs to be right. Common issues we find include:

✓

Duplicate conversion events inflating reported lead numbers

✓

Form submissions counted as conversions with no lead quality filter

✓

No connection between ad platform data and actual sales or bookings

✓

Tracking that was set up once and never checked again since
