Case Studies

The Then, Now, How, Action framework in practice.

Every 64impact engagement follows the same structure: where a business started, where it stands today, how the change happened, and the specific actions behind it.

Illustrative examples. The scenarios below are representative examples built to demonstrate the Then, Now, How, Action approach and are not real client accounts or verified results. They will be replaced with real, verified case studies as campaigns are delivered and clients agree to be featured.
Illustrative Example / B2B Professional Services

Meridian Legal Partners

Google Ads + Tracking
Then

Inconsistent enquiries

Relying on referrals, with a Google Ads account running broad keywords and no clear cost-per-lead target.

Now

A predictable pipeline

A steady flow of qualified enquiries tracked from click through to signed client, with a clear cost-per-lead the firm could plan around.

How

Rebuild and CRM linking

Account restructured around practice areas, negative keyword list built out, and enquiries linked to the firm's CRM for real conversion tracking.

Action

What changed

Wasted spend on unrelated searches cut, landing pages rebuilt per practice area, and budget reallocated weekly toward the best-converting campaigns.

−38%
Cost per qualified enquiry
2.4x
More sales-ready enquiries
90 days
To full account rebuild
"

We finally had a number we trusted. Once that was in place, the rest of the decisions got a lot easier.

Illustrative example — Marketing Director, professional services firm
Illustrative Example / Ecommerce & DTC

Bloom & Co. Skincare

Meta Ads + Analytics
Then

Rising ad costs, flat sales

A single Meta ad creative running for months, with no retargeting audience and no view of true blended ROAS.

Now

A repeatable testing cycle

Fresh creative in rotation, a warm retargeting audience converting at a much lower cost, and accurate revenue tracking back to each campaign.

How

Tracking first, then scale

Conversions API implemented to fix under-reported sales, audiences segmented by funnel stage, and a weekly creative testing schedule introduced.

Action

What changed

Underperforming ad sets paused, budget shifted to the best-converting creative, and a retargeting campaign launched for cart abandoners.

4.9x
Blended ROAS
+52%
Revenue growth, quarter on quarter
−21%
Cost per purchase
"

The tracking fix alone changed how we saw the account. We'd been making decisions on numbers that weren't even right.

Illustrative example — Founder, DTC skincare brand
Illustrative Example / Local & Multi-Location Services

Harlow Fitness Studios

Google Ads + Meta Ads
Then

No consistent lead channel

Three studio locations relying on word of mouth, with an old Google Ads account that hadn't been touched in over a year.

Now

Steady trial sign-ups

A consistent flow of trial membership leads across all three locations, each tracked back to the specific studio and campaign.

How

Location-specific campaigns

Separate campaigns built per studio location, paired with location-specific landing pages and call tracking for phone enquiries.

Action

What changed

Budget reallocated toward the best-performing location, underperforming ad copy replaced, and Meta Ads added to support Google Search demand.

212
Trial sign-ups per month, up from 34
−44%
Cost per trial sign-up
3
Studio locations covered
"

We went from hoping people would find us to actually being able to plan growth around a number we could rely on.

Illustrative example — Owner, multi-location fitness business
