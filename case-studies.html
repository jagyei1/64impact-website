Case Studies

The Then, Now, How, Action framework in practice.

Every 64impact engagement follows the same structure: where a business started, where it stands today, how the change happened, and the specific actions behind it.

Real client work. The case studies below reflect real 64impact client engagements, published with each client's permission. Results are described directionally rather than as exact reported figures.
Real Client / Ecommerce & Fashion Retail

ikan

Meta Ads + Google Ads
Then

Inconsistent ecommerce performance

A distinctive product and clear niche positioning in menswear, but paid advertising performance was inconsistent and revenue wasn't scaling predictably.

Now

A stronger, more efficient revenue channel

A steadily growing ecommerce revenue channel with a stronger return on ad spend and a repeatable system for deciding which products and audiences to back.

How

Product, audience and creative fit

Focused on finding the intersection of best-performing products, the right audience and creative that communicated the brand properly, rather than simply increasing spend.

Action

What changed

Audited existing Meta and Google campaigns, refined prospecting and retargeting, introduced new lifestyle-led creative, improved Shopping feed optimisation, and reallocated budget toward the best-performing campaigns.

Outcome: A significant, sustained increase in online revenue and a stronger return on ad spend.
Real Client / Healthcare

MiCode

Google Ads + Tracking
Then

Volume without quality

Enquiries were coming through Google Ads, but a meaningful share weren't genuinely qualified, and cost per lead was under pressure.

Now

More enquiries that actually convert

A substantially higher volume of qualified enquiries each month, with a healthier qualification rate and a lower cost per lead.

How

Optimising for qualified enquiries

Shifted optimisation away from raw lead volume and toward genuinely qualified enquiries, rebuilding targeting and tracking around the services most likely to convert.

Action

What changed

Rebuilt the Google Ads account structure around high-intent search terms, introduced service-specific landing pages, improved conversion tracking, and refined geographic targeting.

Outcome: A substantial increase in qualified monthly enquiries, alongside a lower cost per lead.
Real Client / Sports & Youth Academies

Halfspace Academy

Meta Ads + Google Ads
Then

Spend without proportional results

A consistent monthly advertising budget, but lead volume and cost per lead weren't where they needed to be.

Now

More leads from the same budget

A markedly higher volume of leads from the same monthly spend, with cost per lead falling significantly.

How

Fixing the leak, not the spend

Identified where leads were being lost between ad click and form submission, tightened targeting, and simplified the path from ad to enquiry.

Action

What changed

Audited historical campaign data, removed low-intent traffic and underperforming keywords, simplified lead forms, and shifted budget toward the highest-converting campaigns.

Outcome: A significant increase in monthly lead volume from the same advertising budget, with a lower cost per lead.
Real Client / Sports & Football Academies

Grasspitch

Meta Ads + YouTube Ads
Then

Inconsistent lead quality

An acquisition funnel that wasn't clearly separating cold prospects from warmer retargeting audiences, leading to inconsistent lead quality.

Now

A predictable flow of better leads

A more predictable, higher-quality flow of qualified leads each month, with a lower cost per qualified lead.

How

Rebuilding around intent

Rebuilt the acquisition funnel around audience intent, separated cold prospecting from retargeting, and aligned landing-page messaging with the specific offer.

Action

What changed

Rebuilt the Meta and Google campaign structure, introduced audience segmentation, created dedicated landing pages, implemented improved conversion tracking, and reallocated budget toward the highest-quality lead sources.

Outcome: A significant increase in qualified monthly leads and a meaningfully lower cost per qualified lead.
