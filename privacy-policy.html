Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data 64impact collects when you use this website, why, and what rights you have over it. Last updated: 15 August 2026.

Who we are

64impact is a founder-led performance marketing consultancy operated by Jude Agyei. For any question about this policy or your data, contact info@64impact.com.

Information we collect

We only collect personal data that you choose to give us. This happens in two ways:

  • Booking a call through our scheduling tool, Cal.com — typically your name, email address and any notes you add.
  • Emailing or calling us directly — whatever you include in that message, such as your name, email address, phone number or details about your business.

We do not run a newsletter sign-up, on-site contact form, or e-commerce checkout, so we do not collect payment details or build marketing lists from this site.

Cookies and third-party services

This site uses a small number of third-party services that may set cookies or make requests to external servers:

  • Cal.com — powers the "Book a call" widget. Loading the widget and using it to book a call may set cookies from cal.com to make scheduling work. See Cal.com's own privacy policy for how they handle this data.
  • Google Analytics — helps us understand how visitors use this site (pages viewed, approximate location at country/city level, device type, how you found us). This only loads if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner — choosing "Necessary only" keeps it switched off. Google acts as a data processor for this information; see Google's privacy policy. We don't use Google Analytics for advertising or remarketing, and we don't combine it with any other data source to identify you personally.
  • Google Fonts — this site loads the Fraunces and Inter typefaces from Google's font servers, which involves your browser making a request to Google.

At present, 64impact.com does not use Meta Pixel or any advertising or retargeting tracking. If that changes in future, this policy and the cookie notice shown on the site will be updated first.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry, schedule and hold your call, and follow up about the services you asked about. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking information only for as long as it's needed to respond to you or, if you become a client, for the duration of that working relationship plus a reasonable period afterwards for accounting and legal purposes.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to ask us what personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive a copy in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, email info@64impact.com. If you're not satisfied with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or our services change. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
