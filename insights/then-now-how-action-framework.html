The Then, Now, How, Action framework explained

How 64impact structures every engagement, and why naming the starting point matters as much as the result.

Jude Agyei · Founder, 64impact · Growth

Every 64impact engagement is structured around the same four questions: where did a business start, where does it stand now, how did that change happen, and what were the specific actions behind it. It's a simple structure, but it does something most reporting doesn't: it makes the starting point part of the story.

Then: the honest starting point

It's tempting to skip straight to results, but a result only means something in context. A cost-per-lead of £40 is a strong outcome if the starting point was £90, and a weak one if the starting point was £25. Naming the starting point honestly, including what wasn't working, is what makes the "now" credible.

Now: the measurable transformation

This is the current state, described in terms that can actually be measured: lead volume, cost per lead, revenue, qualification rate, whatever the business's real goals are. Not platform activity like impressions or clicks, but outcomes that connect back to the business.

How: the strategic thinking

Between "then" and "now" sits a decision about what needed to change and why. This is the strategic layer: whether the problem was structural (account organisation, tracking accuracy), creative (fatigue, messaging), or budgetary (spend allocation, bidding strategy). Getting this right matters more than any individual tactic.

Action: what was actually implemented

Strategy without specific action is just a plan. This is the concrete list: campaigns rebuilt, tracking fixed, landing pages created, budget reallocated, creative tested. It's the part that turns "here's what we think should happen" into "here's what we did."

Why this structure matters

A lot of marketing reporting shows a result without showing the work, which makes it hard to tell whether an outcome was earned through genuine strategy or just favourable circumstances. Structuring every engagement around Then, Now, How, Action keeps the process accountable: the starting point is on record, and the actions taken are specific enough to evaluate, not just claim credit for.
