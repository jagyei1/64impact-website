Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions, answered.

Do you work with businesses that have never run paid advertising before?
Yes. We work with both businesses launching paid advertising for the first time and businesses looking to improve existing campaigns. If you are new to paid media, we help you build the right foundations — from choosing the right platforms and setting up accurate tracking to creating campaigns designed around your business goals. Our aim is to make paid advertising simple, transparent and measurable, so you can invest with confidence.
Do you only work with businesses that already have large advertising budgets?
Not necessarily. We work with ambitious businesses at different stages of growth. However, paid advertising works best when businesses have enough budget to gather meaningful data, test effectively and optimise campaigns. We typically recommend businesses have the ability to invest at least £1,500–£3,000 per month in advertising spend. If you're unsure whether paid advertising is right for your business, we can help you assess the opportunity.
Which advertising platforms do you manage?
We specialise in performance-focused paid media campaigns across Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram), LinkedIn Ads and YouTube Ads. The right platform depends on your customers, industry and growth objectives. We focus on choosing the channels most likely to generate a positive return rather than recommending platforms unnecessarily.
How quickly can I expect to see results?
Every business is different, and results depend on factors such as your industry, offer, budget, competition and customer journey. Some improvements can be identified within the first few weeks, but meaningful optimisation usually requires enough data to test and refine campaigns properly. Our focus is on building sustainable performance rather than chasing short-term results that cannot be maintained.
What happens when I start working with 64impact?
We begin with understanding your business, customers and objectives. From there, we review your current marketing activity (if applicable), identify opportunities, create a tailored strategy and implement the campaigns and tracking required to measure performance. Once campaigns are live, we continuously monitor, test and optimise to improve results over time.
Do I need a website or landing page before running ads?
A strong customer journey is essential for successful advertising. If you already have a website or landing pages, we can review and optimise them to improve performance. If you are launching paid advertising for the first time, we can advise on what is needed to ensure your campaigns have the best chance of converting visitors into leads or customers.
Can you guarantee a specific number of leads or sales?
No. No reputable marketing agency can guarantee specific results because performance depends on many factors outside of advertising alone. What we can guarantee is a structured approach based on data, testing and continuous optimisation to maximise your chances of achieving your goals. We focus on improving the factors we can control: targeting, messaging, tracking, campaign structure and conversion performance.
Do I need to sign a long-term contract?
We believe long-term partnerships should be built on results and trust. Our approach is designed to create sustainable growth, with regular communication, transparent reporting and ongoing optimisation to ensure campaigns continue delivering value. We'll always recommend the approach that makes the most sense for your business rather than locking you into unnecessary commitments.
How much does it cost to work with 64impact?
Every business has different requirements depending on goals, advertising budget and the level of support needed. Our partnerships are tailored around your growth objectives and the complexity of your campaigns. Book a free growth strategy call and we'll discuss your goals, current situation and whether 64impact is the right fit for your business.
