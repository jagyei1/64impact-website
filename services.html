Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions, answered.

Do you work with businesses that have never run paid advertising before?
Yes. We work with both businesses launching paid advertising for the first time and businesses looking to improve existing campaigns. If you are new to paid media, we help you build the right foundations: from choosing the right platforms and setting up accurate tracking to creating campaigns designed around your business goals.
Do you only work with businesses that already have large advertising budgets?
Not necessarily. We work with ambitious businesses at different stages of growth. However, paid advertising works best when businesses have enough budget to gather meaningful data, test effectively and optimise campaigns. We typically recommend businesses have the ability to invest at least £1,500–£3,000 per month in advertising spend.
Which advertising platforms do you manage?
We specialise in performance-focused paid media campaigns across Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram), LinkedIn Ads, YouTube Ads and TikTok Ads. We focus on choosing the channels most likely to generate a positive return rather than recommending platforms unnecessarily.
How quickly can I expect to see results?
Every business is different, and results depend on factors such as your industry, offer, budget, competition and customer journey. Some improvements can be identified within the first few weeks, but meaningful optimisation usually requires enough data to test and refine campaigns properly.
What happens when I start working with 64impact?
We begin with understanding your business, customers and objectives. From there, we review your current marketing activity, identify opportunities, create a tailored strategy and implement the campaigns and tracking required to measure performance.
Do I need a website or landing page before running ads?
A strong customer journey is essential for successful advertising. If you already have a website or landing pages, we can review and optimise them to improve performance. If you are launching paid advertising for the first time, we can advise on what is needed to convert visitors into leads or customers.
Can you guarantee a specific number of leads or sales?
No. No reputable marketing agency can guarantee specific results because performance depends on many factors outside of advertising alone. What we can guarantee is a structured approach based on data, testing and continuous optimisation to maximise your chances of achieving your goals.
Do I need to sign a long-term contract?
We believe long-term partnerships should be built on results and trust. We'll always recommend the approach that makes the most sense for your business rather than locking you into unnecessary commitments.
How much does it cost to work with 64impact?
Every business has different requirements depending on goals, advertising budget and the level of support needed. Book a free growth strategy call and we'll discuss your goals, current situation and whether 64impact is the right fit for your business.
